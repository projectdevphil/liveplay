Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Liveplay Help Center. Below you will find answers to common inquiries regarding our service operations, troubleshooting playback issues, and general usage guidelines.

Is Liveplay free to use?

expand_more

Yes, Liveplay is 100% free. We are committed to providing open access to information and entertainment for Filipinos without any subscription fees or hidden charges.

Is Liveplay safe to watch?

expand_more

Yes, Liveplay is safe. We operate under a strict privacy policy that requires no account registration or logins. We do not collect or store personal identifiable information (PII) or IP addresses. Streams are rendered locally on your device using secure client-side media engines like Google's Shaka Player.

Why is Liveplay exclusively for the Philippines?

expand_more

Our platform is strictly configured and restricted to users within the Republic of the Philippines to match the geographic parameters of the indexed broadcast directories. Any attempt to bypass this geographical limitation using VPNs or proxy networks will result in automatic restriction of access.

Why does Liveplay have limited channels?

expand_more

We deliberately maintain a highly selective selection of channels rather than an exhaustive catalog. By focusing only on the most-watched local and international networks, we are able to maintain a stable, high-performance directory for our core audience.

Can I request a specific channel?

expand_more

No, channel requests are no longer accepted. Our channel lineup has been officially finalized to prioritize long-term link maintenance, stream quality, and overall platform stability.

Why is a channel buffering or not loading?

expand_more

Buffering is typically caused by local network instability or high traffic on the source server. Since Liveplay acts as an indexer and does not host the streams directly, we cannot control the stability of the source broadcast. We recommend checking your internet connection using our Speed Test tool.

Do I need to register an account?

expand_more

No registration is required. We prioritize accessibility and privacy, allowing you to simply visit the platform and begin watching your favorite content immediately.

Does Liveplay have a standalone app?

expand_more

No. Unfortunately, our dedicated Android App (.APK) version has been discontinued. This decision was made to comply with Google's updated Android policies regarding the installation of external APK files. Liveplay is now fully optimized as a responsive web-based platform accessible directly through your mobile browser.

Is Liveplay still available for Smart TVs?

expand_more

No, our dedicated web-based Liveplay for TV site has been discontinued. We recommend accessing the platform through standard web browsers on your mobile devices, tablets, or personal computers instead.

Is this service legal?

expand_more

Liveplay strictly operates as a directory/search index for publicly accessible media links found on the internet. We do not host, upload, or pirate content on our servers. For full legal details, please review our Terms of Service and Disclaimer.
