Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Liveplay Help Center. Below you will find answers to common inquiries regarding our service operations, troubleshooting playback issues, and general usage guidelines.

Is Liveplay free to use?

expand_more

Yes, Liveplay is 100% free. We are committed to providing open access to information and entertainment for Filipinos without any subscription fees or hidden charges.

Why is a channel buffering or not loading?

expand_more

Buffering is typically caused by local network instability or high traffic on the source server. Since Liveplay acts as an indexer and does not host the streams directly, we cannot control the stability of the source broadcast. We recommend checking your internet connection using our Speed Test tool.

Do I need to register an account?

expand_more

No registration is required. We prioritize accessibility and privacy, allowing you to simply visit the platform and begin watching your favorite content immediately.

Can I request a specific channel?

expand_more

Yes, we welcome community feedback. You may submit channel suggestions via our feedback threads on PHCorner. Please note that we can only index channels that have publicly available stream URLs.

Is this service legal?

expand_more

Liveplay strictly operates as a directory/search index for publicly accessible media links found on the internet. We do not host, upload, or pirate content on our servers. For full legal details, please review our Terms of Service and Disclaimer.
