Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

Welcome to Liveplay. This Privacy Policy outlines how Project Dev ("we," "our," or "us") manages and processes information on the Liveplay platform. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy and ensuring a highly secure, non-intrusive environment.

1. Data Minimization & Non-Collection Policy

We operate under a strict principle of data minimization. Liveplay is completely free and does not require account registration or user login. We do not collect, store, or process Personal Identifiable Information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

We utilize local browser storage and essential preference configurations strictly to remember user state settings. For detailed information regarding our use of cookies and how to manage your privacy settings, please consult our dedicated Cookie Policy.

2. Transient IP Processing (Geographical Filtering)

Because Liveplay is restricted to users within the Philippines, we process your connection's IP address upon landing on the site to determine your location.

This IP assessment is strictly transient: the geographic country code is checked in-memory, and the IP address is immediately discarded without being logged, stored, or linked to your activity. We do not keep logs of IP addresses or network request histories.

3. Database Aggregation (Top 10 Rankings)

To populate the "Top 10 Live TV Channels" popularity index, our platform registers when a stream is selected. To protect the integrity of the leaderboard and prevent artificial view manipulation, our database secures these logs using a cryptographic one-way SHA-256 hash of the client's public IP address inside Cloudflare's D1 SQLite Database.

This hash is irreversible, ensuring we cannot reconstruct your original IP address, while allowing us to identify and record unique visits accurately. This analytics process is entirely aggregated. Most importantly, this tracking is completely optional and respects your preferences; if you opt out of Analytics & Performance cookies, no view event or IP hash is recorded.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Data Practices

Liveplay utilizes specific third-party components to deliver core functionality. These services operate under their own data policies:

  • Google's Shaka Player: Our platform integrates the Shaka Player library to render and decode media streams. This utility runs entirely client-side within your browser and does not collect or transmit personal user data to Google or to our servers.
  • Netflix's Fast.com API: Our integrated Speed Test utility queries Fast.com to calculate connection speeds. Your interactions with this utility are subject to Netflix's data practices, which can be reviewed in the Fast.com Privacy Policy at https://help.netflix.com/legal/fastprivacy.

Our service may also provide links to external third-party streams. If you click on an external link, you will be directed to that host. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

5. Compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

As a platform developed and operated in the Philippines, we respect the guidelines outlined in the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Because our service relies entirely on anonymized data and transient processing, and does not collect, manage, or retain personal data, standard database rights (such as data portability, correction, or erasure) are non-applicable, as there is no personal data in our custody to retrieve or delete.

6. Children's Privacy

Our platform does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or local age of majority). Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not maintain profiles or records belonging to underage visitors.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, clarifications, or technical inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, you may contact Project Dev at support@projectdev.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect updates in our systems, infrastructure, or applicable legal regulations. Updates are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
