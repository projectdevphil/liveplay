Cookie Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Liveplay ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar storage technologies. By continuing to use our platform, you acknowledge and consent to our use of cookies in accordance with these guidelines.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. In addition to standard cookies, we utilize Local Storage (localStorage) inside your browser to remember certain configuration states, such as your customized consent choices, so you do not have to re-enter them on every visit.

2. How We Use Cookies

We classify our cookies into two distinct categories to guarantee a transparent and compliant experience:

A. Essential / Necessary Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies and local storage states are absolutely critical for the platform to load and function. They cannot be disabled through our banner options:

  • Consent Management: We save a record of your cookie choice (Accept, Reject, or Customize) in your local storage as cookie_consent to respect your preferences.

B. Analytics & Performance Cookies (Optional)

These features help us evaluate how channels are used, enabling us to build our dynamic leaderboard features:

  • Top 10 Live TV Channels: If authorized, we register when a stream is watched to increment popularity indexes. This system uses hashed IP addresses (SHA-256) to verify uniqueness and prevent cheat cycles, keeping personal IPs entirely anonymous and protected.

3. Respecting Your Privacy Preferences

We believe in absolute data control. If you choose to Reject All or turn off Analytics & Performance tracking via our configuration options, the tracking protocols are completely bypassed on the client side. Your stream activity will not count toward the Top 10 popularity metrics, and no security IP hashes will be created.

4. How to Manage or Update Settings

You can adjust, clear, or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time. Because we store consent in your browser's local storage, clearing your browser's cookies and site data will trigger the Cookie Consent Banner to reappear on your next visit, letting you reset your preferences.
