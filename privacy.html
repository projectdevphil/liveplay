Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 29, 2026

Welcome to Liveplay. This Privacy Policy outlines how Project Dev ("we," "our," or "us") manages information on the Liveplay platform. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy and ensuring a highly secure, non-intrusive streaming environment.

1. Data Minimization & Non-Collection Policy

We operate under a strict principle of data minimization. Liveplay is completely free and **does not require account registration or user login**. We do not collect, store, or process Personal Identifiable Information (PII), such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

2. Geographic Restrictions & Location Processing

Liveplay is exclusively available to users accessing the service from within the Philippines. To enforce this geographic restriction and secure our platform against unauthorized access, we automatically evaluate your connection's IP address upon landing on the site.

This IP assessment is strictly transient: the geographic country code is checked in-memory, and the IP address is immediately discarded without being logged, stored, or linked to your activity. Users attempting to bypass these restrictions, including through the use of Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) configured to Philippine servers, will be restricted by our automated systems.

3. Aggregated Analytics & Channel Rankings

To power our Top 10 Live TV Channels feature, we track general popularity metrics. When you select and stream a channel, our system increments an anonymous popularity counter. This transaction is stored and calculated using Cloudflare's D1 SQLite Database.

These analytics are entirely aggregated and anonymous. We do not associate channel views with unique browser fingerprints, device identifiers, or IP addresses. We only track the total volume of stream hits to maintain a real-time popularity ranking.

4. Third-Party Integrations & External Links

To optimize performance and deliver additional utilities, Liveplay integrates third-party services that operate under their own respective privacy standards:

  • Media Playback Engine: We utilize Google's Shaka Player library to manage media streaming compatibility. Shaka Player runs entirely client-side within your browser and does not transmit personal user data back to Google or our servers.
  • Network Diagnostics: Our integrated Speed Test feature is powered by the Netflix Fast.com API, which allows you to check your network throughput. When using this feature, your interaction is subject to Netflix's data practices. You can review the Fast.com Privacy Policy at https://help.netflix.com/legal/fastprivacy.

Note: The Stream Tester utility has been permanently discontinued and its shortcuts have been removed from the platform.

Furthermore, Liveplay contains links to external live streams and sister networks. We have no authority over, and assume no liability for, the content, privacy structures, or operations of external third-party sites. We advise reviewing the privacy policies of any external destination you visit.

5. Compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

As a platform developed and operated in the Philippines, we respect the guidelines outlined in the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Because our service relies entirely on anonymized data, transient processing, and does not collect or retain personal data, standard database rights such as data portability, correction, or erasure are non-applicable, as there is no personal data in our custody to retrieve or delete.

6. Children's Privacy

Our platform does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect information from children under the age of 13 (or local age of majority). Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, we do not maintain profiles or records belonging to underage visitors.

8. Changes to This Policy
