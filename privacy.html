Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 12, 2025

1. Information Collection

Liveplay is committed to protecting your privacy. We operate on a strict no-log policy regarding user identity. We do not require user registration, and we do not collect personal identification information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

2. Advertisements

We may use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit our website (e.g., pre-roll ads). These companies may use cookies or similar technologies to provide relevant advertisements based on your visits. These cookies do not contain personal information.

3. Aggregate Data & Top 10 Ranking

To generate the Top 10 Live TV Channels list, we utilize aggregated usage data (view counts). This data is processed anonymously via Redis (Vercel) to rank channels by popularity. No individual user activity is tracked or stored in a manner that can identify you personally.

4. Usage Data & Analytics

Like most modern websites, we may collect non-personal technical data that your browser sends automatically. This may include:

  • Device type (Mobile, Desktop, Tablet)
  • Browser version
  • Time and date of visit

This data is used solely for system optimization and debugging purposes.

5. Local Storage & Preferences

We use local storage technologies to enhance your experience (e.g., saving your "Most Watched" preferences or theme settings). This data is stored entirely on your specific device and is not transmitted to our backend servers.

6. Integrated Tools & Third-Party Links

Our service provides links to external streams and sister platforms (such as Stream Tester by Project Dev). Please be aware that these sites operate independently.

Additionally, Liveplay features an integrated Speed Test utility powered by Project Dev and utilizing Netflix's fast.com API. Use of this feature is subject to Netflix's data practices. You can review Netflix's Fast.com Privacy Policy at https://help.netflix.com/legal/fastprivacy.

If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service or legal requirements. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
